Yongchang Hao

Yongchang Hao is a Ph.D. student at the University of Alberta working with Professor Lili Mou. His research interests widely lie in machine learning, including language models, reinforcement learning, and optimization. His long-term research goal is to build intelligent systems that are compute-efficient and scalable.
